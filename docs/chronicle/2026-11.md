## 2026-11-17 · F-051 Single-deal matches are the fastest way to earn XP (found in F-005 review). Check whether XP for single deals should be reduced.

Single-deal matches (a match of one deal, which is the fastest to finish) used to pay the same flat match XP as a full match, so one deal could earn more XP per hour than any full game. Those deals now earn 0.2 times the normal match XP, rounded. A seeded simulation measured the problem first: single deals earned about 3 to 5 times more XP per deal than 62- or 100-point matches. After the change they earn less than a 62-point match. The 0.2 figure is a proposed product choice, marked [review] in `docs/design.md`, and a single deal can no longer buy a level.
