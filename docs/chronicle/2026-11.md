## 2026-11-15 · F-003 [Employer #1, offline P1] Settings screen: separate music and effects toggles, notifications toggle (local reminders only, no push service), contact support link, send feedback/suggestions

Settings now has separate Music and Sound effects toggles that survive a restart, replacing the single sound switch. It also has a Notifications toggle that schedules one daily local reminder at 20:00 on native builds, plus Contact support and Send feedback links that open an email to the support address, which is also shown as text. The reminder does not use exact alarms, and the new settings migrate from older saves. The support address and the 20:00 reminder time were chosen in the spec and need a person to confirm them.
